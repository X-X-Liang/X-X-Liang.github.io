A warm welcome to our new visiting doctoral student, Qinghan Hou, from the Ocean University of China! Qinghan is joining our group for a 3-month research stay (04.2026-06.2026), supported by the Sino-German Mobility Program. Under my supervision, he will be working on the spatiotemporal modeling of laser-induced plasma formation in aqueous media, further advancing the research on ultrashort laser pulse energy deposition.
