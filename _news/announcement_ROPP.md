I am incredibly proud to announce that our comprehensive original research article, **"Laser-induced plasma formation and cavitation in water: from nanoeffects to extreme states of matter,"** has just been published online in *Reports on Progress in Physics* (IOP Publishing). 

With a journal Impact Factor of 20.4, this publication represents a major milestone for our research group at the Institute of Biomedical Optics. Co-authored with my esteemed colleagues Norbert Linz, Sebastian Freidank, and Alfred Vogel, this extensive 55-page report synthesizes years of theoretical and experimental advancements.
