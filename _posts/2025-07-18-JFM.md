I am thrilled to share that our 2022 paper, **"Comprehensive analysis of spherical bubble oscillations and shock wave emission in laser-induced cavitation,"** has been officially recognized as one of the [Top 10 cited articles contributing to the 2024 Impact Factor](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/jfm-top-10-cited-articles-contributing-to-2024-impact-factor) of the *Journal of Fluid Mechanics* (*JFM*).

This recognition highlights the ongoing relevance and impact of our theoretical and numerical investigations into laser-induced bubble dynamics within the fluid mechanics community. I am deeply grateful to my co-authors—Norbert Linz, Sebastian Freidank, Günther Paltauf, and Alfred Vogel—for their invaluable contributions and collaboration on this extensive study.
